  El proyecto AFarEdge está diseñado para cerrar la brecha entre la industria europea de Sistemas y Componentes Electrónicos (ECS) y los agricultores, optimizando el flujo de trabajo para la evolución de los componentes agrícolas y aprovechando la experiencia europea en desarrollo tecnológico. Al incorporar capacidades de procesamiento local, el proyecto aborda desafíos como la asignación de tareas, la seguridad y la eficiencia en el uso de recursos, aspectos fundamentales para los agricultores. AFarEdge aborda estos obstáculos mediante el uso de técnicas modernas de aprendizaje automático, que permiten el modelado adaptativo y el procesamiento descentralizado, reduciendo así la dependencia de costosos sistemas centralizados de prueba y validación.

The AFarEdge project is designed to bridge the gap between the European ECS industry and farmers by optimizing the workflow for the evolution of agricultural components, leveraging European development expertise. The initiative addresses the pressing question of how to balance ambitious Green Deal objectives with European food security, particularly in the face of climate disruptions, geopolitical tensions, and dwindling farm profitability. To meet these challenges, AFarEdge leverages breakthrough semiconductor innovations, originally developed for automotive and industrial automation, which hold great potential for agriculture. These technologies can be cost-effectively customized to support European farms in fulfilling Green Deal requirements while maintaining viable livelihoods.

AFarEdge emphasizes the importance of AI-enhanced data processing along the Edge to Cloud Continuum, which offers the most promising outcomes in agricultural applications. By deploying local processing capabilities, AFarEdge addresses challenges like task allocation, security, and resource efficiency, all crucial for farmers. Additionally, AI-driven technologies enable privacy-conscious, distributed intelligence, such as federated learning, which enhances device accuracy while preserving data security.

The project also aims to address the technical challenges posed by agriculture's unique environment. Harsh conditions and limited connectivity require durable, energy-efficient solutions for edge data processing. Additionally, the lack of calibration data for new sensors can hinder their widespread adoption. AFarEdge addresses these obstacles by utilizing modern machine learning techniques, enabling adaptive modeling and decentralized processing that reduce reliance on costly, centralized testing.
