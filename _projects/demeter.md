## About the Project

**DEMETER** is a flagship **Horizon 2020** initiative focused on transforming the European agri-food sector through **interoperable, data-driven smart farming solutions**.  
With deployments across **18 countries** (15 of them EU Member States), DEMETER integrates IoT technologies, advanced data analytics, and interoperable models to support more **sustainable, efficient, and resilient agricultural production**.

The project leverages:

- **IoT sensors and devices** (38,000+ deployed)  
- **Integrated data from diverse sources** (open data, satellite, legacy systems, geographical systems)  
- **A farmer-centric approach** across the entire agri-food value chain  
- **Interoperability standards** shaping the Agricultural Information Model

Through these pillars, DEMETER enhances **resource management**, supports **data-driven decision-making**, and contributes to the EU’s broader goals of **sustainable agriculture**, **food security**, and **environmental protection**.

---

## Objectives

DEMETER aims to:

- Deploy **interoperable smart farming platforms** across **20 pilots** and **25 sites**.  
- Involve and support **6,000 farmers**, covering varied production sectors (dairy, meat, vegetables, fruit, arable crops).  
- Demonstrate **real-world interoperability** between IoT technologies and data-driven services.  
- Integrate multiple data sources (IoT, open data, satellite imagery, legacy systems) into a **unified Agricultural Information Model**.  
- Ensure **security, privacy, and confidentiality** across the full agri-food value chain.  
- Enable “**doing more with less**” through optimisation, sustainable practices, and decision-support tools.  
- Foster an **open, sustainable IoT ecosystem** that promotes innovation and new business opportunities in European agriculture.

---

## Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) Contribution

UPM participated in DEMETER through the **GRyS Research Group**, supporting the development of the technical foundations enabling smart, secure, and interoperable agricultural systems. Core contributions included:

- **IoT architectures and semantic interoperability**, supporting standardisation within the Agricultural Information Model.  
- **Data analytics and decision-support methodologies** for sustainable resource management.  
- **Integration of heterogeneous data streams**, including legacy systems and sensor networks.  
- **Testing and validation** within selected pilots, ensuring robustness and user-centred design.  
- **Dissemination and knowledge transfer**, supporting best practices and community adoption across the EU.
