Are you an **Engineering graduate** passionate about **Edge Computing**, **Distributed AI**, and **Smart Farming**? The **Next Generation Networks and Services Group (GRyS)**, within the **Department of Telematics and Electronic Engineering** at the **Technical University of Madrid (ETSIST-UPM)**, is seeking a **First Stage Researcher (R1)** to join the **AFarEdge Project – Aggregated Intelligence for Farming at the Edge**.

## Main Tasks
- **Edge AI Architecture Development**: Contributing to the specification and development of an Edge Computing architecture to enable edge AI and distributed learning.
- **Semantic Middleware Integration**: Participating in the specification and development of a semantic middleware layer to integrate heterogeneous devices and diverse data models.
- **Edge Deployment Automation**: Collaborating on the design and development of components that facilitate edge solution deployment through MLOps and DevOps approaches.
- **Scientific Dissemination and Documentation**: Supporting the drafting of technical reports and scientific articles on project progress.
- **International Collaboration**: Actively participating in working meetings and coordination with European consortium partners.

## Requirements
- **Master's degree** in **Engineering (ICT)**.
- Strong knowledge of **AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning models**, with background in **Federated Learning** highly desirable.
- Proven knowledge and experience in developing **Edge Computing** applications and architectures.
- Proficiency in **microservices architectures**, containerization (**Docker**), and open-source **CI/CD workflows** (DevOps/MLOps).
- Understanding of **data models, ontologies**, and knowledge representation languages.
- Experience with communication protocols (**REST, gRPC, MQTT**) and full-stack development (databases, object storage, real-time dashboards) for monitoring distributed deployments.
- Experience in **system modeling, simulation, and verification/validation testing** aligned with standards such as ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119.
- **Fluent spoken English** for presentations and meetings, alongside strong skills in drafting formal and academic technical documentation.
- Candidates currently enrolled in a **PhD in Engineering** will be highly valued.
- Demonstrated ability to work on applied research combining software and AI for real-world deployments.
- Capacity to collaborate in multidisciplinary environments and with international research teams.
- **Languages**: Spanish (Advanced), English (Intermediate).
