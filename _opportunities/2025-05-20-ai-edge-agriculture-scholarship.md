Are you a Telecommunications Engineering student passionate about **AI**, **Edge Computing**, and **Precision Agriculture**? The **Next Generation Networks and Services Group (GRyS)** at the **Technical University of Madrid (ETSIST-UPM)** is offering a **3-month research scholarship** to support an exciting applied research project!

---

## Project Title
**Development of AI-based Intelligent Systems for Edge Computing in Precision Agriculture**

## Location
**ETSIST-UPM**, C/Nikola Tesla, Madrid, Spain

## Key Details
- **Working Hours**: 25 hours/week  
- **Gross Monthly Grant**: €500  
- **Expected Start Date**: June 16, 2025  
- **Duration**: 3 months  
- **Application Deadline**: **June 1, 2025 at 12:00 PM (Central European Summer Time - CEST)**

---

## Key Tasks
- Conducting scientific literature reviews
- Gaining knowledge on edge intelligence and distributed AI in agri-tech
- Designing and developing a proof of concept for edge-based agricultural systems (e.g. autonomous fleet management, AI-driven crop data analysis)
- Preparing documentation (reports, manuals)
- Delivering oral presentations

---

## Eligibility
- Enrolled students completing their final year project in **Telecommunications Engineering**  
- Preferably with a focus on **Electronic Communications Engineering**  
- Must be a student at **ETSIST-UPM**
