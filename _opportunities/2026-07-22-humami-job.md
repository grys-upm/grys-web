Are you an **Engineering graduate** passionate about **Semantic Web technologies**, **Knowledge Graphs**, and **AI-driven data integration**? The **Next Generation Networks and Services Group (GRyS)** at the **Technical University of Madrid (ETSIST-UPM)** is seeking a **First Stage Researcher (R1)** to join the **HumAMI Project – Human-inspired Advanced Machine Intelligence for accelerating European AI innovation, cooperation and industrial adoption**.

## Position
**Research Engineer – M.Sc. in Telecommunications Engineering (Telematics Specialization)**

## Location
**ETSIST-UPM**, C/Nikola Tesla, s/n, 28031 Madrid, Spain

- **Gross Annual Salary**: €41,300
- **Estimated Start Date**: September 2026
- **Contract Duration**: Until **August 31, 2029**

---

## Main Tasks
- Contributing to the **design and development of semantic interoperability frameworks** based on ontologies and knowledge graphs for heterogeneous data ecosystems.
- Assisting in the **research and implementation of AI agent-based approaches** for automated ontology alignment and semantic integration across distributed systems.
- Participating in the **design and validation of semantic data management architectures** to enable reliable, interoperable information exchange.
- Supporting **data engineering activities** for the integration, transformation, and semantic enrichment of heterogeneous data sources in AI-driven environments.

---

## Requirements
- **Master's degree** in **Engineering (ICT)**.
- Solid background in **Semantic Web technologies** (RDF, OWL, SPARQL, SHACL) and **ontology engineering** methodologies and tools (e.g., Protégé).
- Experience with **Knowledge Graph architectures**, graph databases/triplestores (e.g., GraphDB, Virtuoso, Neo4j), and semantic data integration/mapping tools (e.g., RML, R2RML).
- Familiarity with **AI-driven approaches** for semantic integration, such as Multi-Agent Systems, NLP techniques, or ontology alignment algorithms.
- Solid grounding in **software engineering practices**: UML modelling, simulation, and testing under ISO/IEC/IEEE 29119.
- Strong **technical writing, documentation, and presentation skills** in English and Spanish.
- Candidates currently enrolled in a **PhD in Engineering** will be highly valued.
- Demonstrated ability to work on applied research combining software and AI for real-world deployments.
- Capacity to collaborate in multidisciplinary environments and with international research teams.
- **Languages**: Spanish (Advanced), English (Intermediate).
