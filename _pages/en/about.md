**GRyS (Next-Generation Networks and Services)** is a research group at **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)** dedicated to exploring, designing and developing the technologies that will define the connected systems of tomorrow.

We work at the intersection of **cyber-physical systems (CPS)**, the **Internet of Things (IoT)**, **cooperative robotics** and **distributed software architectures**, seeking to make complex systems more intelligent, adaptive and resilient.

GRyS is a space where **seasoned researchers and emerging scholars collaborate**, combining solid academic grounding with the flexibility and creativity needed to tackle today's technological challenges.

## Mission

To advance the understanding and development of **next-generation networked and intelligent systems**, bridging the gap between research and practice through **innovation, collaboration and openness**. We aim to produce not only excellent scientific outcomes but also **technological solutions that contribute to society**, enhance sustainability and enable new forms of digital interaction.

## Vision

A world where connected systems work **seamlessly, responsibly and sustainably**, empowering people and organisations to make better decisions. As part of UPM's research ecosystem, GRyS seeks to be recognised as a **reference group in cyber-physical and networked systems**, fostering impactful collaborations and open research that transcends disciplinary boundaries.

## Our story

GRyS has been a **research group at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid since 2009** (consolidated in 2011), growing within the School of Telecommunication Systems and Engineering (ETSIST) as a multidisciplinary team focused on the design and evolution of advanced communication and computing systems.

From the beginning, our ambition has been to understand how distributed systems could become more efficient, adaptive and intelligent, long before the term *cyber-physical systems* became widely used. In the same spirit we have embraced the Internet of Things, cooperative robotics, semantic middleware and cloud–edge computing, and have taken part in, and led, major European initiatives such as the H2020 **SWARMs** and **AFarCloud** projects.

## Looking ahead

We continue to expand our collaborations through the **OpenTech Lab**, our space for applied experimentation and open technological development. With new challenges on the horizon, from sustainability to AI-driven systems, GRyS remains committed to connecting knowledge, innovation and people.
