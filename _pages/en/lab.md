The **OpenTech Lab** is an open, modular experimentation environment for exploring the **orchestration of cooperative missions among autonomous and semi-autonomous systems**. It integrates **vehicles, sensors, actuators and data sources** into a unified control and data-management framework, bridging research and real-world applications.

## Vision

The lab embodies **open innovation** and **interdisciplinary collaboration**: a space where researchers, students and industry partners can **experiment, develop and validate** technologies that make distributed systems more autonomous, resilient and intelligent.

It relies on **semantic interoperability**, so that heterogeneous components (from robots to sensor networks) exchange information through shared vocabularies; on **large language models** for natural-language querying of mission data; and on **cybersecurity** as a foundational requirement.

## Open collaboration

The OpenTech Lab is open to research groups, students and organisations interested in **testing, integrating or extending** its capabilities.
