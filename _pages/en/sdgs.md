At GRyS, we align our research and innovation activities with the **United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development**. Through our projects, collaborations and educational initiatives, we contribute to several Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by promoting **technological innovation**, **sustainable digital infrastructures** and **knowledge transfer** that benefits society and the environment.
