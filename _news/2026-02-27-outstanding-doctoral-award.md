The Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) has awarded the prestigious **Outstanding Doctorate Award** to several of its most distinguished doctors, including our colleague Mario San Emeterio. This accolade recognizes academic excellence and the exceptional quality of research conducted during their doctoral studies.

To be eligible for this award, recipients must have defended their thesis with the highest distinction of "cum laude" and demonstrated significant and original contributions to their field of study. Additionally, the evaluation considers publications in high-impact scientific journals and other merits related to their research.

This recognition not only highlights the effort and dedication of the awardees but also underscores UPM's commitment to high-quality research and the training of academic and scientific leaders.
