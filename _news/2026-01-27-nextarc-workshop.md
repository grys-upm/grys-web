On January 27, 2026, [GRyS](https://grys.etsist.upm.es), hosted the **NexTArc Workshop 2026** in Madrid. The event took place at the Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería y Sistemas de Telecomunicación (ETSIST) – Universidad Politécnica de Madrid.

The workshop brought together researchers and collaborators of the NexTArc project to discuss advances in next-generation network architectures, edge computing, and intelligent service management. The agenda included technical presentations, demonstrations, and working sessions aimed at strengthening collaboration among project partners.

The workshop served as a valuable platform for sharing results, identifying synergies, and planning the next steps of the project.
