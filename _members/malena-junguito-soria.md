**Malena Junguito** completed her undergraduate studies at the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es), within the *Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería y Sistemas de Telecomunicación (ETSIST)*, where she pursued a **Double Degree in Electronic Communications Engineering and Telematics Engineering**.

During her time at UPM, she joined the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group as an **intern**, contributing to projects related to **next-generation communication systems** and **emerging network technologies**.

Her collaboration with GRyS provided her with early research experience and a strong foundation in applied telematics and communications engineering.
