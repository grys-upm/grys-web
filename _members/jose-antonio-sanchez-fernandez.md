## A Lifelong Commitment to the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid
**José-Antonio Sánchez-Fernández** earned his **Master Degree in Physical Sciences** from the [Universidad Complutense de Madrid](https://www.ucm.es) in **1989**.  
He soon joined the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es), where he devoted his entire professional life with dedication, rigor, and a deep sense of service.

Throughout his distinguished career, José-Antonio held several key academic and administrative positions, including:

- **Deputy Director for Research, Postgraduate Studies, and External Relations** (*Subdirector de Investigación, Postgrado y Relaciones Externas*) at the former *Escuela Universitaria de Ingeniería Técnica de Telecomunicación (EUITT)* — now the *Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería y Sistemas de Telecomunicación (ETSIST)*.  
- **Deputy Director for Economic Affairs** (*Subdirector de Asuntos Económicos*) at the same institution.  
- **Acting Director** (*Director en funciones*) of the EUITT.  
- **Adjunct to the Vice-Rector for Academic Management and Faculty Affairs** (*Adjunto al Vicerrector de Gestión Académica y Profesorado*) at UPM.  
- **Academic Secretary** (*Secretario Académico*) of the ETSIST.

His leadership and commitment were instrumental in the development of the School and in strengthening UPM’s academic and research structures.

---

## Research and Group Involvement
José-Antonio was also a committed member of the Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS) research group, where he collaborated on projects and initiatives related to **networked systems and telematic services**.  
His analytical perspective and institutional experience enriched the group’s multidisciplinary approach and research management capabilities.

---

## Legacy
José-Antonio passed away in **September 2024**, leaving behind a profound legacy of **dedication, professionalism, and humanity**.  
He is remembered by colleagues, students, and friends for his generosity, calm leadership, and tireless support for the university community.

> *“Your wisdom, kindness, and example will continue to guide those of us who had the privilege of working alongside you.”*
