**Vicente Hernández Díaz** is an *Associate Professor* at the [Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería y Sistemas de Telecomunicación](https://www.etsist.upm.es), [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es).  
He obtained his **M.Sc. in Electronic Engineering** from the *Universidad de Alcalá* in 2013 and has been a member of the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group since 2005.

His research interests include **ubiquitous computing**, the **Internet of Things (IoT)**, and **network resilience**.

He has participated in several European research projects, including [DEMETER]({{ 'projects/demeter' | ref }}), [AFarCloud]({{ 'projects/afarcloud' | ref }}) (*Precision Agriculture*), and [SWARMs]({{ 'projects/swarms' | ref }}) (*Underwater Collaborative Robots*).
