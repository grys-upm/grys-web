**Gregorio Rubio Cifuentes** holds a **Technical Telecommunication Engineering degree** from the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es) (1988) and a **Degree in Documentation** from the *Universitat Oberta de Catalunya* (2008).  
He served as an *Associate Professor* in the [Department of Telematic Engineering and Electronics (DTE)](https://www.etsist.upm.es/escuela/departamentos/DTE) at UPM until his **retirement in January 2025**.

A dedicated member of the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group since 2009, Gregorio focused his work on **semantics and ontologies** for **Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN)**, **service management**, and the **Internet of Things (IoT)**.

Throughout his academic career, he contributed to several European research projects such as *DiYSE* and *ACCUS*, and collaborated in the management and coordination of major initiatives like *DEMETER* and *AFarCloud*.
