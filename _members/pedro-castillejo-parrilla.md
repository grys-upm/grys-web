**Pedro Castillejo** is an *Associate Professor* at the [Department of Telematic Engineering and Electronics (DTE)](https://www.etsist.upm.es/escuela/departamentos/DTE), [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es), where he earned his **Ph.D. in Telecommunications** in 2015.  
He is a member of the [GRyS](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group.

His work spans **cyber-physical systems**, **wireless networks**, **communication protocols**, **security algorithms**, and **embedded middleware**.

Pedro has participated in numerous European projects such as *ESNA*, *DIYSE*, *LIFEWEAR*, *DEMANES*, *e-GOTHAM*, *I3RES*, *SWARMs*, and *AFarCloud*.  
He has co-authored over **25 scientific publications** in indexed journals and conferences and serves as an **invited lecturer** in several master’s and doctoral programs.
