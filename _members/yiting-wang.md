**Yiting Wang** is a *Research Assistant* and *Ph.D. student* at the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es), where she obtained her **M.Sc. in the Internet of Things (IoT)** in 2021.  
She is a member of the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group, working within the *Systems and Services Engineering for the Information Society* doctoral program.

Her research focuses on **IoT systems**, **distributed services**, and **intelligent network architectures**, with a special interest in **semantic interoperability** and **data-driven automation**.
