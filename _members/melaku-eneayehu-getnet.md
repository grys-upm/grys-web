Melaku Eneayehu was born in Dembecha, Ethiopia. He received a bachelor degree in computer science in July 2015 from Dilla University (Ethiopia). He received a master degree in computer science and engineering on December 30, 2018, from Parul University (India), with a thesis entitled “Brain Tumor Detection and Classification from MRI Using Supervised and Unsupervised Machine Learning Algorithms.”

From July 2015 to August 2024, Melaku worked as a Graduate Assistant, Assistant Lecturer, and Lecturer in the Computer Science Department at Dilla University.

Since September 2024, he has been a Lecturer in the Software Engineering Department at Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU), currently on study leave.
Since March 2025, he is a Ph.D. student and a member of the Internet of Things (IoT) Lab Group at the Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Parma, under the FutureData4EU project.
