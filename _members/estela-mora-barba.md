**Estela Mora Barba** is an *Predoctoral Researcher* in the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) group at the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es).  
She holds a **dual degree in Electronic Communications Engineering and Telematics Engineering**, and a **master's degree in Internet of Things.s**

Her work focuses on the **design and validation of intelligent fleet management systems** for **autonomous agents**, as well as the development of **embedded AI architectures** for **resource-constrained IoT devices**.

Her research interests include **cyber-physical systems**, **artificial intelligence at the edge**, and **energy-efficient IoT architectures**.
