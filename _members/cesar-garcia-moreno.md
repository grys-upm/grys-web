**César García Moreno** completed his **Double Degree in Communication Electronics and Telematics** at the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es) in 2024.

He joined the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group as an **intern**, where his responsibilities include contributing to the development of GRyS' web pages and exploring the feasibility of implementing a **LoRa network** for precision agriculture applications.

César was recognized for his outstanding academic work as part of the **Best Final Year Projects (TFG) of 2024** awarded by the *Colegio Oficial de Ingenieros Técnicos de Telecomunicación (COITT)*, in its XVIII edition of the *Premios Futuro de las Telecomunicaciones*.

In a historic first for the COITT, he received **first place in two different categories** in the same year—an unprecedented achievement reflecting the innovation and impact of his work.

The awarded projects focused on cutting-edge fields:  
- **Communication and processing system** for obtaining horizon elevation  
- **Design and development of a three-axis stabilized pointing system** based on microprocessors for obtaining self-leveled and magnetically oriented images
