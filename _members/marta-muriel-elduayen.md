**Marta Muriel Elduayen** began her research career in **2021**, after completing her *Master’s Degree in Development of Applications and Services for Mobile Devices*.

As a member of the [Next-Generation Networks and Services (GRyS)](https://grys.etsist.upm.es/) research group, she participated in the **European project [AFarCloud](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/783221)**, contributing to research on smart and connected agricultural systems.

She is currently an **Assistant** at the Department of Telematic Engineering and Electronics (DTE), [Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería y Sistemas de Telecomunicación (ETSIST)](https://www.etsist.upm.es), where she teaches subjects on **mobile networks** and **programming**.

Her ongoing research is focused on the **optimization of Case-Based Decision Support Systems (DSS)**, exploring how intelligent models can enhance decision-making processes in dynamic and data-driven environments.
