**Santiago Higuera de Frutos** holds a **Ph.D. in Civil Engineering** from the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)](https://www.upm.es).  
Since 1992, he has combined his work in **construction and infrastructure** with active participation in **open-source software** and **GIS** communities.

He has been a *Professor and researcher* at UPM since 2014, currently based at *Teleco Campus Sur*.  
Santiago is the author of *[Programación en Rust](https://www.garceta.es/catalogo/libro.php?ISBN=978-84-1728-988-1&idd=12)* and *[Programación y Métodos Numéricos para Ingeniería con MATLAB y Octave](https://www.garceta.es/catalogo/libro.php?ISBN=978-84-1903-435-9&idd=12)* (Editorial Garceta).

His research interests include **driving simulators**, **road layout and design**, and **open-source GIS development**.
