## Overview

**Tech-Hub 6G Madrid** took place on **November 12, 2025**, at the **ETSI Sistemas de Telecomunicación – Campus Sur, UPM**, Madrid.

The event was organized to **showcase and connect the technological capabilities of companies, SMEs, and research groups** from the Madrid region, focusing on the development of the sixth generation (6G) of mobile communication systems and services.

The day featured a plenary session with four invited talks, two discussion panels, and a continuous exhibition of projects and technological capabilities accessible throughout the day.

---

## Opening and Key Attendees

The inauguration included:

- Viceconsejera de Universidades, Investigación y Ciencia de la Comunidad de Madrid  
- Decana del Colegio Oficial de Ingenieros de Telecomunicación  
- Vicerrector de Innovación, Emprendimiento y Transferencia, UPM  
- Directora de la ETSI Sistemas de Telecomunicación  
- Company executives and leading regional researchers

---

## GRyS Participation

Members of **GRyS** attended the event, engaging with the project presentations and the technological exhibition. The event also provided a valuable opportunity to connect with other research groups, industry partners, and stakeholders in the 6G ecosystem.
