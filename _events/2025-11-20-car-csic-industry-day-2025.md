## Overview

**Industry Day 2025**, organized by **CSIC** and the **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)**, brought together researchers, industry representatives, startups, and public administrations in a key event focused on public–private collaboration in **R&D&I in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics**.

The event showcased a wide range of technological demonstrators and featured an expert agenda addressing topics of strategic interest for innovation, technology transfer, and industrial collaboration.

Members of **GRyS** participated in the event, contributing to demonstrations and discussions related to artificial intelligence, robotics, and applied research.

## Programme Highlights

The agenda combined live demonstrations, expert talks, and networking opportunities, including:

- AI and robotics demonstrators in healthcare, agriculture, transportation, construction, and autonomous systems
- Sessions on collaboration mechanisms with CSIC and UPM
- Presentations on R&D&I tax incentives and support instruments
- Contributions from innovation hubs, startup ecosystems, and digital innovation initiatives

---

## GRyS Participation

GRyS participated in the demonstrator sessions through the **OpenTech Lab**, presenting work related to:

- Managing autonomy through data and control
- Cooperative missions involving autonomous and semi-autonomous vehicles
- Applied AI and robotics solutions with potential for technology transfer
