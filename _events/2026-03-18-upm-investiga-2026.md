## Overview

**UPM Investiga 2026** – the first global research fair organised by the **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)** – took place on **March 18–19, 2026** at the **Central de Diseño in Matadero Madrid**. The event aimed to bring UPM’s research activity closer to society while fostering cooperation with the business sector and public administration, creating a space for the exchange of ideas and new collaboration opportunities.

Members of **GRyS** (Next Generation Networks and Services Group) participated actively, showcasing their latest advancements in intelligent systems and AI-powered tools within the **“AI and digitalisation”** booth.

## Programme Highlights

The two‑day fair featured:

- Thematic booths covering AI, digitalisation, advanced manufacturing, biomedicine, energy, and mobility
- Live demonstrations of research prototypes and technology transfer cases
- Networking sessions between research groups, companies, and public administrations
- Workshops on innovation funding and collaborative R&D instruments

---

## GRyS Participation

GRyS participated with a dedicated space within the **“AI and digitalisation”** booth, demonstrating several technological advancements:

- **Smart Fleet Manager** for autonomous vehicle coordination
- **Device for generating prescription maps** for precision agriculture
- **Interactive tool for secure natural language querying** of databases

The GRyS team members in attendance included **Estela** and **Mario** (both at the booth during the event), **Xi Tao** (assisted on the morning of the first day), and **Néstor** (present from 15:30 until the end at 22:00 on March 18).

The event provided valuable networking opportunities, with initial conversations already exploring potential synergies with other research groups.
