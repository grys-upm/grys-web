## Overview

We are pleased to invite NexTArc project partners to participate in the next **NexTArc Project Workshop**, which will take place on **January 27, 2026**, at the **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid – Campus SUR**.

This workshop will provide an excellent opportunity for partners to meet in person, exchange perspectives, and engage in joint discussions around the project’s progress and next steps. The event is designed to encourage collaboration, shared reflection, and the exchange of ideas among consortium members.

---

## Practical Information

### Date and Time

- **Date:** January 27, 2026  
- **Time:** 09:00 – 17:00 (full working day)

Participants are encouraged to arrive in Madrid on **January 26**.
